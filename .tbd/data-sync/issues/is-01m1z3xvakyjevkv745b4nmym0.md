Found while routing the squares explainer's relation glyphs (U+2265, U+2248, U+2192; also U+2264, U+2260) to a shipped face: the Source Sans 3 woff2 kpress vendors is fontsource's latin subset of 231 code points and has none of them, though upstream Source Sans 3 carries them all at the sans's own weight and axis. The squares page now draws them from a 684-byte KaTeX_Main-Bold subset with a 70% size-adjust on a unicode-range face of the sans family, which is a workaround. Vendor a second small subset of Source Sans 3 (the variable face, and the static print instances at the token weights) covering the mathematical relation and arrow ranges documents use (measured list, or U+2190-21FF and U+2200-22FF), declared as extra unicode-range faces of Source Sans 3 Variable and of the print family, generated by instance_sans.py or a sibling tool with --check, with source package and sha256 in the fonts README. Then the squares workaround (RELATION_SIZE_ADJUST and relation_face_css in render_explainer.py) goes under a squares bead.
