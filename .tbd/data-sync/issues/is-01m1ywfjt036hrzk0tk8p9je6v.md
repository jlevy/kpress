Chromium's PDF writer draws Source Sans 3 Variable at any non-default weight as Type3 outline paths, so printed sans text is not a font in the PDF; viewers that smooth embedded text (Preview) leave the paths thin and the sans reads lighter than PT Serif and the mathematics beside it. Measured: outlines at 410 are the right weight (0.083 em stem, same as screen); with Quartz font smoothing on, embedded fonts gain 5-20% ink and Type3 paths gain none. Ship static instances at the weights kpress's sans contexts request (370, 400, 550, 600, 650, 700; normal and italic) generated by devtools/instance_sans.py into static/fonts, declared as the 'Source Sans 3' family in a generated static/css/print-fonts.css under @media print, registered in assets.py after print.css; print.css puts 'Source Sans 3' ahead of 'Source Sans 3 Variable' in the sans stack under print, behind a --kpress-host-font-sans-print hook. Screen is unchanged. Makefile lint and lint-check run the generator's --check.
